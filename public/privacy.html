RatioShot Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026 · App: RatioShot (com.ratioshot.camera)

1. What we collect

RatioShot has no account or sign-in and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses or phone numbers.

  • Photos: Photos you take are processed on your device only and saved to your device's photo library (album "RatioShot"). They are never uploaded to any server.
  • Settings: Your selected aspect ratios are stored on the device only.
  • Advertising identifier: The banner ad at the bottom of the app is served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP) and device information to serve and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: used only to take photos.
  • Photos/media: used only to save your photos to an album (Android 9 and below).
  • Internet: used only to show ads.

3. Third parties

Your photos and settings are not shared with anyone. Ad-related data is handled by Google AdMob under its own policies. Users in the EEA/UK are asked for ad consent when the app starts.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

5. Deleting data

Uninstalling the app removes its settings. Saved photos can be deleted from your device's photo app. The advertising ID can be reset or personalized ads turned off in your device settings.
